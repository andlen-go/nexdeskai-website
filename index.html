Features

  • Trainable AI

    Teach NexdeskAI from what you already have. Start with your documents, FAQs, website pages, and Business Rules, then add or retire content as your policies change.

    • Documents
    • FAQs

  • AI that keeps learning, with your approval

    NexdeskAI reviews past conversations and suggests new knowledge, FAQs, and rules. A person approves each suggestion before it goes live, so your content stays current and stays in your control.

  • Business Rules

    Configure how the AI handles action requests. Decide when it asks for details, which action it may start, and when a person takes over.

    See Business Rules

  • Multi-tenancy

    Organize your support team around different brands, branches, or products. Each one gets its own setup, content, and rules, all under one platform.

  • Replies that sound natural to your customers

    Set the language and tone to fit your business. For Filipino customers, that can mean a clear English answer, a Filipino reply, or a natural Taglish exchange with the right level of courtesy.

Business Rules

Describe what should happen. Give your team the final say.

Business Rules tell NexdeskAI when to ask for details, what action it may start, and when a person should take over. With AI-assisted setup, a support lead can describe a procedure in plain language. AI helps turn that instruction into a draft rule for the team to review, test, and approve before it goes live.

How the rule would work

  1. Payment reported and course locked
  2. Ask for the reference
  3. Request verification
  4. Enrollment team reviews and decides Human review
What a customer needs What NexdeskAI can start What your team owns
Report a problem Collect the details and file a report or open a ticket Investigate and resolve the issue
Update a routine detail Update a permitted field, such as a preferred callback time Set what can be changed and handle exceptions
Get a proposal Collect requirements, draft a proposal, and send it after approval if sending is enabled Approve pricing, terms, and what goes out

Example workflows depend on which rules and actions are enabled for your team.
