NexdeskAI

NexdeskAI is an AI customer conversation product: a shared inbox, answers from the business's own content, lead qualification, and handoff to a person. The team works one queue instead of switching between channel apps. When a person needs to take over, the thread is already in that queue.

What NexdeskAI includes

  1. 01

    Multi-channel inbox

    WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, and site chat come into one shared inbox.

    A teammate can continue a thread that started on another channel, and the customer does not have to repeat the message.

  2. 02

    Grounded answers

    Answers are drawn from the business's own URLs, documents, and FAQs.

    A reply uses that supplied material as its source. Content the business has not provided is not used as a source.

  3. 03

    Lead qualification and handoff

    Leads are qualified in the conversation, then handed to a person.

    The thread can gather what the customer wants and how to reach them before a person steps in, and that person gets the same conversation.

  4. 04

    CRM pipeline

    A pipeline shows where each conversation stands.

    Each conversation has a stage, from the first reply through later follow-up, so the team can see what is waiting, active, or done.

NexdeskAI at nexdeskai.com is this product. It is not NexaDesk, NexusDesk AI, or Nexdesk.io.
